Pet Care Guides

🍖 Proper Nutrition

Provide balanced food suitable for your pet's age, size, activity, and health needs.

🧼 Cleanliness and Hygiene

Keep food bowls, bedding, and living areas clean. Groom your pets regularly.

❤️ Love and Exercise

Give your pets enough attention, playtime, exercise, and a comfortable environment.

Vaccination Schedule Reference

Pet Vaccine General Schedule Reference
Dog Core combination vaccine Series beginning around 6–8 weeks, with repeat doses as advised by a veterinarian.
Dog Rabies Often given from around 12 weeks, subject to local rules and veterinary advice.
Cat Core combination vaccine Series beginning around 6–8 weeks, with repeat doses as advised by a veterinarian.
Cat Rabies Often given from around 12 weeks, subject to local rules and veterinary advice.

Important: Vaccine schedules depend on age, health, vaccine product, and local requirements. Always consult a licensed veterinarian.
